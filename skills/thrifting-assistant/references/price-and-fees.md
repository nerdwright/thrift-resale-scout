# Price, fees, and verdict

US marketplace fees, checked November 2026 against eBay's selling-fees help page and Poshmark's and Vinted's published US fee rules. Fees change. Treat every formula here as an estimate and say so.

## Comps

- Search the style code first (it usually gives an exact match), then brand + model + size + condition.
- Prefer **SOLD** evidence: eBay sold/completed listings, Poshmark sold listings.
- If only active listings turn up, label the range **ASKING only (est.)** and estimate sold at about 60–80% of asking.
- Match comps on brand + model + size + condition. A size outlier (very small or very large) or a flaw can halve the price; say so.
- Say when comps are thin (fewer than about 3 close matches) or when there are many unsold listings for the same item (slow sell-through).
- Sneakers: also check StockX or GOAT for a deadstock reference, then discount for used condition, box, and laces/insoles.
- Marketplace pages may block automated fetching. If a page doesn't load, say so and fall back to search snippets plus the quick links. Never fill gaps from memory.

## Net per platform (US)

Defaults assume the seller offers free shipping on eBay (label baked into price) and the buyer pays shipping on Poshmark and Vinted.

| Platform | Net estimate | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| eBay, most clothing and shoes | price × 0.853 − $0.40 − label | 13.6% final value fee on the total sale, which includes sales tax (13.6% × 1.08 ≈ 14.7%), plus a $0.40 per-order fee ($0.30 if the order is $10 or less) |
| eBay, men's or women's athletic shoes sold at $150+ | price × 0.914 − label | 8% final value fee on the total including tax (≈ 8.6%), no per-order fee |
| Poshmark, sale $15+ | price × 0.80 | 20% commission. Buyer pays shipping |
| Poshmark, sale under $15 | price − $2.95 | Flat fee. Buyer pays shipping |
| Vinted US | price | No seller fee. Buyer pays a protection fee and shipping |

Default eBay labels: $8 apparel, $14 shoes. Use the user's numbers when given.

Platform notes for BEST PLATFORM:

- **eBay:** largest buyer pool; best for men's, workwear, vintage, sneakers, and anything searched by style code.
- **Poshmark:** strong for women's mall and premium brands; 20% fee bites on higher prices.
- **Vinted US:** keeps 100% of the price, but a smaller US buyer pool means slower sales. Best for $15–$45 mall brands where speed matters less.

## Multiple and verdict

- **Multiple** = best realistic net ÷ tag price. "Realistic" means the price that platform's comps support, not the highest price on any platform.
- **BUY:** multiple ≥ 3× **and** net ≥ $15 **and** confidence Medium or High.
- **MAYBE:** the numbers are close, comps are thin, confidence is Low, no live price check was possible, or one more photo would decide it.
- **PASS:** multiple < 3×, condition kills the value, or the item is frequently faked and can't be checked in-store.

Use the user's own multiple and minimum net when they give them.

## Worked example

Illustrative numbers only; always search for real comps. Patagonia Synchilla Snap-T, men's M, good condition, tag $6.99. Suppose sold comps cluster at $40–$50; use $45.

- eBay: 45 × 0.853 − 0.40 − 8 = **$29.99**
- Poshmark: 45 × 0.80 = **$36.00**
- Vinted: **$45** if it sells at $45, but expect a slower sale
- Best realistic net: Poshmark $36 → 36 ÷ 6.99 = **5.2×** → BUY

PROFIT line format:

> **PROFIT:** eBay ~$30 · Posh ~$36 · Vinted ~$45 (slow) · **5.2×** on $6.99 (3× / $15 rule, fees as of Nov 2026)
